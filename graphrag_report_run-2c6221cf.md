## WHY

**Motivation**
  - FTX pursued actions leading to bankruptcy due to poor private key management, lack of formal board meetings for major decisions, inadequate cash management systems, and questionable auditing practices
  - Wilson behaved this way because the risk and return were too asymmetric, with the risk of losing all principal outweighing small losses from slippage and fees
  来源：<https://m.huxiu.com/article/736848.html>

**Trigger**
  - FTX 私自挪用 用戶資產
  - FTX暴雷事件 triggered 數十億美元的虧空因私自挪用用戶資產
  - FTX暴雷事件 導致 FTX一夕之間迅速垮塌
  来源：<https://www.twreporter.org/a/ftx-black-swan-event-goblin-mode>

## HOW

**Mechanism**
  - CEXs are trying to regain trust by demonstrating proof of reserve including assets and liabilities
  - DEX and on-chain perpetuals experienced the largest gains in the days following FTX bankruptcy
  - FTX collapsed within a few days leading to bankruptcy liquidation
  - FTX event analysis focuses on underlying principles and mechanisms rather than just what happened
  - Frank adds large DEX, especially derivatives DEX, may need to build an application chain for further growth
  - Institutional investment is a concern for whether it will return to support the crypto industry after FTX collapse
  - Wilson anticipates future trading systems moving entirely on-chain despite difficulties
  - Wilson evaluated risk and preserved assets by spending about 2 hours to close positions and immediately transferring money out
  - Wilson reason for quick exit from FTX risk and return were too asymmetric, with potential total loss of principal outweighing small slippage and fees
  - zk-SNARK is considered as a potential solution for safely and privately proving exchange liabilities
  来源：<https://m.huxiu.com/article/736848.html>
